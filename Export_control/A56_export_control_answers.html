Export-Control & GF180 Answer Key

Transcription-ready answers for the 11-page GlobalFoundries “Export Control & GF180 Configuration Information” intake form. Every value in the exported PDF snapshots (aa, 111, stray Yes toggles) is a placeholder — the answers below are the real ones for dla_engine_chip, cross-checked by an independent export-control / classification / device-inventory review.

dla_engine_chip · A56 / ACV block 1675 × 1110 µm · GF180MCU single-supply 3.3 V · ≤25 MHz INT8 MNIST-GAN accelerator self-classified EAR99

Bottom line

A benign, open-source (Apache-2.0) 180 nm fixed-function digital ASIC — a 16-MAC INT8 inference accelerator (~0.8 GOP/s) with no RF/mmWave, encryption, floating-point, ≥32-bit ALU, processor core, quantum content, or military design intent. Self-classified EAR99.

Two answers are deliberately Yes and honest: EC 12 — the physical chip is subject to the EAR (as EAR99); and EC 7 — it genuinely is a fixed-function ASIC (disclosed; the “for other equipment” clause is not met, so the outcome stays EAR99). It sits ~6 orders of magnitude below the 3A090 advanced-computing threshold, so every advanced-computing / military / encryption item resolves No.

Only the private-party fields — the end user (EC 20) and the authorized signatory block (Page 11) — need your real data. Until those are filled, the Completion check is “Needs follow-up.”

02

Product description & RF / mmWave

GF EC 1a – 1c

GF EC 1a

Briefly describe the product/chiplet/structure and its final end use.

EnterThe product (top cell dla_engine_chip) is a GlobalFoundries GF180MCU (180 nm) fixed-function digital ASIC: a 16-element (4×4) output-stationary INT8 multiply-accumulate inference accelerator with eight 256×8 on-chip SRAM operand buffers, accessed over a 4-wire synchronous serial host interface (SCLK/MOSI/CS_N/MISO). It is a single-supply 3.3 V, fully static device (DC to ~25 MHz, ~125 mW, ~0.4 GMAC/s INT8) with no processor core, no floating-point unit, no ≥32-bit ALU, no RF/wireless, and no cryptography. Its sole final end use is an educational, open-source (Apache-2.0) demonstration of on-chip machine-learning inference: running a small pretrained MNIST handwritten-digit GAN generator to render 28×28 images. Manufactured samples are returned to the design team for laboratory-bench evaluation driven by a 3.3 V MCU/FPGA host and are not incorporated into any commercial product or end item.

States the exact device type, benign parameters and demonstration-only end use — the facts that support the EAR99 self-classification.

GF EC 13 No

Does the item meet or exceed the ECCN 3A090 performance parameters?

Harmonized System classification for your project.

Select8542.31.0070 — System on chip (SoCs). Alternative / fallback: 8542.31.0030 — Other (same heading) if the organizer reads “SoC” strictly as requiring an embedded programmable CPU.

A monolithic digital IC — a processor/controller combined with memories (heading 8542.31). Not a memory (8542.32), amplifier (8542.33), RF/converter (8542.39) or bare part (8542.9).
