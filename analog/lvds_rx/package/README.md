## Integration

- `lef/lvds_rx_x3.lef`: CLASS BLOCK, SIZE 650 x 194.2, OBS on Nwell and Metal1-Metal3; route over the macro on Metal4/Metal5 only. VDD / VSS are the Metal3 straps (connect the core grid with Metal4 stripes and via3). RXk_P / RXk_N on the top edge (pad side), EN and RXk_O on the bottom edge (core side).
- `lib/`: one Liberty file per corner (tt_025C_3v30, ss_125C_3v00, ff_n40C_3v60), blackbox without timing arcs; RXk_O max_capacitance 30 fF and max_transition 1 ns (the verified load and edge).
- `verilog/lvds_rx_x3.v`: `SYNTHESIS` blackbox, otherwise a behavioural model (v0: an idle input holds the last value). Do not tie the power pins in RTL; drive the pad pins through wires in testbenches.
- `spice/lvds_rx_x3.spice`: the LVS reference the layout matched (gf180mcuD device names); `gds/lvds_rx_x3.gds`: DBU 0.001.
- Keep EN LOW until VDD >= 2.0 V; capture RXk_O through synchronizers (asynchronous to the core clock).

## Known limits (`KNOWN_LIMITS.json`)

- **esd_secondary**: UNQUALIFIED: the secondary ESD cell (ppolyf_u 5 x 1.8 um, 151 ohm tt, + 40 um2 clamp diodes to VDD and VSS) has no HBM / CDM / latch-up data; no ESD pass/fail is claimed anywhere (layout/lib/README.md, PLAN_V2 L6)
- **failsafe**: not guaranteed (v0): idle, terminated or shorted inputs hold a stable state with no oscillation and no pulse < 2 ns (TB7, all 45 corners x 7 VIC, MC N = 100), but which state is not defined; the Verilog model holds the last value
- **latchup**: unverified: no latch-up deck beyond the foundry DRC; guard rings and taps per the T8 library
- **power_off_input_behaviour**: the pad's own ESD diode to DVDD conducts from about 0.58 V when DVDD = 0 (TB0, pad_asig_5p0); the macro does not defend unpowered inputs
- **en_and_supply**: no under-voltage lockout: 'VDD < 2.0 V -> outputs LOW' holds only with EN = 0; the integrator must hold EN low until VDD >= 2.0 V (decision D-008)
- **low_supply_off_state**: with EN = 0 and VDD below about 1.2 V no stage of the output chain (a NAND and three inverters) conducts and RXk_O float to about a third of VDD: up to 0.363 V extracted on a 50 ns supply ramp, 0.316 V on the 1 us spec ramp at ss/-40C (14 mV under the 0.33 V LOW limit), 0.254 V on the 100 us ramp (decision D-019; TB8 cases 0, 6, 7); every output is back under 10 % of VDD from about 1.2 V on. The logic that reads RXk_O must be held in reset, and EN low, until VDD >= 2.0 V (D-008)
- **package**: assumed package and board: source 50 ohm per side, L 3 nH, C 0.7 pF per pin, swept L {1, 5} nH x C {0.5, 1.5} pF in TB12 (netlist/input_network.spice); not measured
- **pad_slot_x**: inferred pad-slot x positions (spec/pins.yaml, UNVERIFIED organizer item): a different slot moves only the pin row
- **hysteresis_window**: 6-35 mV input-referred (ladder R3 rung 1, decision D-009; the handoff asked 8-25 mV)
- **saturation_margin**: the PMOS input pair may run up to 100 mV into triode at VIC 0.05 V at fast / hot corners (decision D-012); gain, delay and eye are verified there after extraction (gate TBL, D-012 condition)
- **parasitic_estimate**: the pre-layout netlist carries a layout parasitic estimate calibrated on the T10 extraction (decision D-016); the delivered layout is checked against it by gate TBL
- **timing_arcs**: none in the Liberty (v1): RXk_O are asynchronous and must be captured through synchronizers
- **integration_open**: UNVERIFIED items of the handoff (section 16) that only chip integration can close (PLAN_V2 P7/P8, owner-gated; PLAN_V2 section 9): (15) OpenSTA / OpenROAD handling of clocks and input delays defined on macro output pins, (16) whether RSZ_DONT_TOUCH_RX stops port buffering of the RX inputs, (17) the analog template geometry and slot assignment, (18) the semantics of the LVS keys (organizer items)
